Understanding Your Age Calculation

This calculator works out your age from your date of birth to today. It shows your age in completed years, months, and days.

How to Use It

Select your date of birth, then click the Calculate Age button. Check that the date and year are correct before calculating.

Example

If your date of birth is 1 January 2000, the calculator uses today's date to work out how many years, months, and days have passed.

About the Result
