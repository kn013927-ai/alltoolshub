How to Use the Age Calculator

Enter your date of birth and click the Calculate Age button to find your age in years, months, and days.

Example: Select 1 January 2000, then click Calculate Age.

Understanding Your Age Calculation

This calculator works out your age from your date of birth to today's date. It shows your completed years, months, and days.

About the Result

The result is an age calculation, not an official document. For legal or official purposes, follow the instructions of the organization requesting your age.

Benefits of Using Our Free Online Age Calculator

Our free calculator works directly in your browser. It does not require registration, and your date of birth is not sent to a server.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this Age Calculator accurate?

It calculates your age using the current date and your date of birth, including leap years.

Do you save my date of birth?

No. The calculation happens in your browser, and your date of birth is not stored by this calculator.

Can I calculate another person's age?

Yes. Enter any valid date of birth to calculate the person's age.
