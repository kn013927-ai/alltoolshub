What is a Percentage?

Percentage means "per hundred" or "out of 100". It is a way to express a number as a fraction of 100. The symbol of percentage is %. In our daily life, we use percentage to calculate student marks, shopping discounts, bank interest, profit and loss, and data analysis. For example, if you get 45 marks out of 50, it means you got 90%.

Percentage Formula

Our Percentage Calculator uses a very simple formula to find the percentage:

Percentage (%) = (Your Number ÷ Total Number) × 100

For example, if your number is 30 and total number is 150, then (30 ÷ 150) × 100 = 20%.

Real Life Uses of Percentage

  • Education: To calculate exam marks and results.
  • Shopping: To calculate discount like 20% OFF on clothes.
  • Business: To calculate profit, loss and growth.
  • Finance: To calculate bank interest and taxes.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1: How to calculate percentage of marks?
Divide your obtained marks by total marks and multiply by 100. Example: (450 / 500) * 100 = 90%.

Q2: What is 100%?
100% means complete or full. If you complete 100% of your work, it means you finished all of it.

Q3: How to find 10% of any number?
Just divide the number by 10. For example, 10% of 200 is 200 / 10 = 20.
